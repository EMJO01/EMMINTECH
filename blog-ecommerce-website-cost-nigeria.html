How Much Does an E-commerce Website Cost in Nigeria?

If you're planning to sell products online, one of the first questions you'll probably ask is: "How much does an e-commerce website cost in Nigeria?" The answer isn't one fixed price.

An online store can cost anywhere from a relatively simple setup to several million naira, depending on the number of products, payment system, shipping, integrations, design, and level of customization required.

The important question isn't just "how much does an online store cost?" It's "what does your business actually need?"

Do you actually need an e-commerce website?

Not every business needs a full e-commerce platform on day one.

You may need a Landing Page if...
  • You sell one main product
  • Customers mainly contact you on WhatsApp
  • You are testing demand
You may need a Business Website if...
  • You need credibility
  • You want customers to understand your services
  • Most sales happen through enquiries
You may need E-commerce if...
  • You have multiple products
  • Customers need to browse independently
  • You want customers to place orders online
  • You need structured product/order management
You may need Automation if...
  • Customers constantly ask the same questions
  • Your team manually handles orders
  • WhatsApp conversations are becoming difficult to manage
