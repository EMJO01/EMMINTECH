What the audit checks

Five areas, covering everything between "can someone find you" and "can someone actually become a customer."

01

Google Visibility

  • Google Business Profile
  • Business information
  • Search presence
  • Reviews
  • Local relevance
02

Website

Does the business have a website?
  • Mobile experience
  • Clear offer
  • Contact / CTA
  • Trust signals
  • Basic SEO
03

Conversion

If someone discovers this business today, can they easily become a customer?
  • Clear next step
  • WhatsApp / contact options
  • Product / service info
  • Pricing where appropriate
  • Enquiry / order process
04

Content & Discovery

  • Searchable content
  • Social presence
  • Educational content
  • Information consistency
  • Answers customer questions online
05

Automation

  • Repetitive customer questions
  • Manual order handling
  • Lead follow-up
  • Booking processes
  • Internal repetitive tasks
