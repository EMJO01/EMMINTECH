OrderFlow

Turning WhatsApp from a customer-support inbox into an automated sales system.

View Live Demo See More Projects

01 — The Problem

Many small businesses already use WhatsApp to receive orders — but the process is still largely manual. A customer sends a message, and the business owner has to:

  • Respond
  • Answer product questions
  • Send the catalog
  • Take the order
  • Calculate the total
  • Confirm the order
  • Repeat the entire process for the next customer

That means every additional customer creates more manual work.

OrderFlow was designed around a simple question: what if WhatsApp could handle the repetitive parts of the ordering process automatically?

02 — The Diagnosis

The problem wasn't:

"This business needs AI."

The problem was:

The sales process depended too heavily on manual conversations.

So instead of forcing customers onto another platform or asking them to download an app, the experience stayed exactly where customers already were — WhatsApp. The customer still sends a normal WhatsApp message. The system handles the repetitive work behind the scenes.

03 — The Strategy

Customers were already on WhatsApp, comfortable with it, and using it daily. Changing that behavior would have meant fighting the business's own customers to adopt something new — a new app, a new account, a new habit.

The stronger move was building around behavior that already existed, not against it. That single decision shaped everything that followed.

04 — The Solution

OrderFlow is an AI-powered WhatsApp ordering system. The customer journey becomes:

Customer messages→ AI responds→ Catalog displayed→ Customer chooses→ Order collected→ Order confirmed

OrderFlow handles catalog management, the WhatsApp connection, AI conversations, order taking, and confirmation — with no new app required for the customer.

How It Works

01

Add Your Products

The business uploads its catalog — product names, prices, sizes, and photos. Products can be updated anytime through the dashboard.

02

Connect WhatsApp

The business connects its existing WhatsApp Business number. Customers keep using the same number they already know.

03

Let AI Handle the Conversation

When a customer messages, OrderFlow responds, presents the catalog, understands what they want, and takes the order.

04

Confirm the Order

Once the customer selects what they want, the system confirms the order and captures the necessary details.

What I Learned

"The best solution isn't always the most complicated one. It's the one that removes the biggest bottleneck in the customer's journey."

Customers were already on WhatsApp. So the solution didn't need to change their behavior — it needed to improve what happened after they sent the message. That's the difference between building technology and solving a business problem.
